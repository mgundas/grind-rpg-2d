# Jade Throne: Game Design and Technical Specification

Jade Throne is a 2D browser MMORPG inspired by the classic Korean MMORPG formula: three rival kingdoms, four classes, grinding packs of monsters, giant spirit stones that spawn waves of enemies, risky equipment upgrades, item bonuses, parties, guilds and player trading. It runs fully in the browser, looks polished, and above all **feels good to play**.

Working title: Jade Throne (it can change later; keep the name in one constant).

---

## 1. Pillars

1. **Juicy combat.** Every hit has feedback: hit flash, small knockback, damage numbers, sound, particles, light screen shake on crits. Hitting a pack of 6 monsters at once must feel satisfying.
2. **The grind loop.** Kill packs, break spirit stones, collect drops, upgrade gear, get stronger, move to the next area. Rewards come often and in small amounts, with rare exciting ones.
3. **Gambling on upgrades.** Upgrading +0 to +9 can fail and destroy the item. This tension is a core part of the fun.
4. **Kingdom identity.** Every player belongs to one of three kingdoms with its own color, home map and rivalry.
5. **Smooth multiplayer.** Movement never stutters, other players glide smoothly, actions respond instantly.

---

## 2. Visual direction

- **View:** top-down 3/4 perspective (like classic 2D action RPGs), not isometric.
- **Art:** pixel art based on the **LPC (Liberated Pixel Cup)** standard. Tiles are 32x32. Characters use the Universal LPC spritesheet format with 64x64 frames and layered equipment, so equipping armor or a weapon changes how the character looks.
- **Rendering:** Phaser 4 WebGL, `pixelArt: true`, camera zoom 2 (integer zoom, configurable), smooth camera follow with slight lerp.
- **Polish layer:**
  - Y-sorted depth for all entities and tall map objects (trees, houses).
  - Soft elliptical shadow under every character and monster.
  - Day/night cycle (20 real minutes per full cycle) using a color tint plus point lights for torches, lamps and skill effects via Phaser 4 lighting/filters.
  - Particles for hits, skills, level up (pillar of light), item drops (beam colored by rarity), spirit stone hits (stone shards).
  - Glow filter on rare items on the ground and on +7 and higher weapons.
  - Floating damage numbers: white normal, yellow crit (bigger, with a small bounce), red for damage taken, green for heals.
  - Name plates: player names colored by kingdom; monster names colored by level difference (gray, white, yellow, red).
  - Ambient weather per map (falling leaves, light rain), toggleable.
- **UI:** HTML/React overlay on top of the canvas, styled in a dark lacquered-wood and jade-green theme with gold accents, consistent across all windows. Windows are draggable, remember their position (localStorage), and close with Escape.

### 2.1 Assets and placeholders

The game must be **fully playable before any real art exists**.

- All visuals are loaded through `apps/client/public/assets/manifest.json`, which maps logical keys (for example `char.body.male`, `mob.wild_dog`, `icon.item.sword_01`, `tileset.village`) to file paths.
- If a file is missing, a **procedural placeholder** is used automatically:
  - Characters and monsters: a colored rounded shape with a darker outline, a small arrow showing facing direction, a shadow, and a simple squash-and-stretch walk bob. Color comes from kingdom (players) or monster type.
  - Item icons: a rounded square in the rarity color with the first two letters of the item name.
  - Tiles: generated flat-color tiles with subtle noise (grass, dirt, stone, water, wall).
- Write `ASSETS.md` listing every asset the game expects, its key, its size, and where to get it:
  - Characters: Universal LPC Spritesheet Generator. Layer order: body, legs/pants, feet, torso armor, head/helmet, weapon. Standard sheet layout, rows in this order, each with 4 directions (up, left, down, right): spellcast (7 frames), thrust (8), walk (9), slash (6), shoot (13), then hurt (1 row, 6 frames). Frame size 64x64.
  - Tilesets: LPC tilesets (terrain, village buildings, dungeon).
  - Item icons, skill icons, portraits: will be generated by the human with an image model at 64x64 and placed under `assets/icons/`.
  - LPC art is CC-BY-SA / GPL: the game must have a Credits screen that reads from `assets/CREDITS.md`.

## 3. World and lore (original)

Three kingdoms fight for the Jade Throne:

| Kingdom | Color | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| **Ember** | Red | Fierce, mountainous, fire temples |
| **Gale** | Yellow | Plains and wind, trade-minded |
| **Tide** | Blue | Coastal, mystic, water shrines |

Corrupted **Spirit Stones** fell from the sky and are spreading corruption, mutating animals and raising spirits. Players fight corruption while their kingdoms compete.

### 3.1 Maps (final scope)

| Map | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ember / Gale / Tide Village (x3) | 1 to 15 | Home town: NPCs, shops, blacksmith, warehouse. Surrounded by fields with low-level monsters. |
| Ember / Gale / Tide Highlands (x3) | 15 to 30 | Second kingdom map. Spirit stones appear here. |
| Valley of Echoes | 25 to 45 | Neutral map shared by all kingdoms. PvP allowed between kingdoms. |
| Corrupted Forest | 40 to 60 | Neutral, harder monsters, rarer drops. |
| Spider Cave (dungeon) | 35 to 50 | Instanced per party, boss at the end. |

Phases 2 to 8 use only **one village map plus its field** (Ember Village, used by all kingdoms with tint). More maps come in phase 9.

### 3.2 Map creation

Maps are stored as **Tiled JSON** (`.tmj`) in `packages/data/maps/` so they can be edited later in the Tiled editor. Since you cannot use Tiled, write a script `scripts/generate-maps.ts` that procedurally generates good-looking maps in valid Tiled JSON format:

- Layers: `ground`, `decor` (below entities), `above` (drawn above entities, for tree tops and roofs), `collision` (tile layer), and an object layer `objects` containing: `spawn_player`, `npc` (with `npcId` property), `mob_zone` (rectangle with `spawnGroupId` property), `stone_zone`, `warp` (with `targetMap`, `targetX`, `targetY`).
- Village: roughly 80x80 tiles, central plaza, roads, houses with collision, trees, a river with a bridge, fences, map border of trees/cliffs.
- Fields: roughly 160x160 tiles, organic grass/dirt patches, tree clusters, rocks, clear open grinding areas where mob zones are placed.
- Use seeded randomness so maps are reproducible.

The server reads the same `.tmj` file to build its collision grid, spawns, NPCs and warps.

## 4. Characters

### 4.1 Creation

- An account has up to **4 character slots**.
- On account creation, the player chooses a **kingdom** (one per account, all characters share it).
- Character creation: name (3 to 16 chars, letters/numbers, unique, case-insensitive), class, gender (cosmetic), hair style and color (3 each).

### 4.2 Classes

Each class picks one of two **doctrines** at level 5, which unlocks 6 active skills.

| Class | Weapons | Doctrine A | Doctrine B | Starting stats (VIT/INT/STR/DEX) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Warrior** | Swords, two-handed swords | Blade (offense, AoE) | Iron Body (tank) | 4 / 3 / 6 / 3 |
| **Assassin** | Daggers, bows | Shadow (melee burst, stealth) | Hawk (archer) | 3 / 3 / 4 / 6 |
| **Hexblade** | Swords | Cursed Steel (melee + buffs) | Dark Arts (ranged magic) | 3 / 5 / 5 / 3 |
| **Mystic** | Bells, fans | Dragon (offensive magic) | Grace (healing, buffs) | 3 / 6 / 3 / 4 |

Phase 3 implements basic attacks for Warrior and Assassin. All four classes with doctrines and skills come in phase 6.

### 4.3 Stats

- **VIT:** max HP (+40 per point), defense (+0.5 per point)
- **INT:** max MP (+20 per point), magic attack (+2 per point), magic defense
- **STR:** physical attack (+2 per point for Warrior/Hexblade, +1 for others)
- **DEX:** attack rating (hit chance), evasion, physical attack for Assassin (+2 per point), crit chance (+0.1% per point)

Gain **3 stat points per level** up to level 90 (cap per stat: 90). Level cap: 99.

### 4.4 Formulas (put all of these in `packages/shared/src/formulas.ts`, with tests)

```
xpToNextLevel(L)       = floor(120 * L^2.25)
maxHP                  = classBaseHP + VIT*40 + level*36
maxMP                  = classBaseMP + INT*20 + level*12
physicalAttack         = weaponAttack(+upgrade bonus) + statAttack + level*2
damageRoll             = random between 0.9 and 1.1 of attack
damage                 = max(1, round(damageRoll * attackerAtk - defenderDef * 0.7))
hitChance              = clamp(0.7 + (attackerDEX - defenderDEX)*0.005 + (attackerLvl - defenderLvl)*0.01, 0.4, 0.98)
critChance             = 0.02 + DEX*0.001 + itemCritBonus
critMultiplier         = 2.0
levelPenalty (XP)      = monster 10+ levels below player gives 20% XP; 5 to 9 below gives 60%; above player gives up to +20%
deathXPLoss            = 2% of current level XP (never de-level), only from PvE deaths
HP regen out of combat = 2% max HP every 3s; in combat 0.5% every 3s
MP regen               = 2% max MP every 3s
```

Tune numbers so a new Warrior kills a level 1 monster in 2 to 3 hits, and a level 10 player clears a pack of 5 same-level monsters in about 20 seconds.

## 5. Controls and movement

- **Click to move** (left click on ground), with server-side A* pathfinding over the collision grid. Holding the mouse button keeps walking toward the cursor.
- **WASD / arrow keys** for direct 8-direction movement (sprites show 4 directions; diagonals use the closer horizontal direction).
- **Space** (hold): attack in facing direction. Melee attacks hit **all** enemies in a frontal arc (120 degrees, class-dependent range). This multi-hit is essential to the fun of grinding packs.
- **Left click on a monster**: walk to it and auto-attack until it dies or the player moves.
- **Z**: pick up nearby items. **Auto-loot** option for gold and items the player owns.
- **1 to 4** and **F1 to F4**: two hotbar rows (skills and consumables, drag from skill window or inventory).
- **I** inventory, **C** character/stats, **K** skills, **P** party, **G** guild, **M** world map, **Enter** chat, **Tab** cycle nearby targets, **Esc** close windows / menu.
- Walk speed: 200 px/s. Mounts (phase 9): 300 px/s.

### 5.1 Networking model (important for smoothness)

- Server simulation tick: **20 Hz** fixed (`setSimulationInterval`, 50 ms). State patches at 20 Hz.
- Client renders at display refresh rate.
- **Own character:** client-side prediction. Client sends input messages `{ seq, dir }` or `{ seq, moveTo: {x,y} }`. Both client and server run the **same deterministic movement step** from `packages/shared/src/movement.ts` against the same collision grid. The server includes the last processed `seq` per player; the client reconciles by replaying unacknowledged inputs. Snap only if the error exceeds 64 px, otherwise smooth-correct over 100 ms.
- **Other entities:** buffered interpolation, rendered 100 ms in the past, between the last two received positions. Never teleport except for warps or corrections over 200 px.
- **Interest management:** use Colyseus `StateView` so each client only receives entities within roughly 1.5 screens of their position (around 900 px radius). Recompute visibility every 250 ms, not every tick.
- Combat actions (attack, skill, use item) are sent as messages; the client plays the animation immediately (optimistic), the server confirms hits and damage via messages or state.
- Server validates: movement speed (reject impossible positions), attack cooldown, range, line of sight not required.

## 6. Combat and monsters

### 6.1 Monsters

Data in `packages/data/monsters.json`. Fields: id, name, level, hp, attack, defense, dex, moveSpeed, attackRange, attackCooldownMs, aggroType (`passive` | `aggressive`), aggroRange, leashRange, xp, goldMin, goldMax, dropTableId, spriteKey, color (placeholder), size, isBoss.

**Monster AI** (server, simple state machine): `idle` (wander inside zone) -> `chase` (on aggro) -> `attack` -> `return` (if leash range exceeded, walks back and fully heals). Monsters with no player within 1200 px **sleep** and skip AI ticks.

**Pack behavior:** monsters spawn in **spawn groups** (3 to 7 monsters around a leader). When one member is attacked, the whole group aggroes. Respawn: whole group respawns 30 to 60 s after the last member dies.

Starter content (Ember fields, levels 1 to 15), create at least 10 monster types, for example: Wild Dog, Gray Wolf, Alpha Wolf (leader), Wild Boar, Brown Bear, Bandit Thug, Bandit Archer, Corrupted Deer, Lesser Spirit, Wolf King (field boss, level 15, spawns every 20 minutes).

### 6.2 Spirit Stones

Large stationary rocks with a glowing crack, spawned randomly in `stone_zone` areas (max 3 per zone, respawn 3 to 5 minutes after destruction).

- Huge HP, no attack of its own.
- Every time it loses another 20% of its HP, it **spawns a wave** of 4 to 6 monsters around it.
- On destruction: big shard particle burst, screen shake, drops gold, upgrade materials, a chance for skill books, and items from its drop table.
- Visible from far away with a pulsing light so players race to it.

### 6.3 Drops

- Drop tables in `packages/data/dropTables.json`: list of `{ itemId, chance, minQty, maxQty }`, plus gold range.
- Drops belong to the player (or party) that dealt the most damage for 30 s, then anyone can pick them up. Items despawn after 2 minutes.
- Item on ground shows a beam of light colored by rarity, and a name label when Alt is held.

### 6.4 Death

- PvE death: lose XP per formula, respawn options **Restart here** (after 10 s, with 30% HP) or **Restart in town** (immediately, full HP).
- Death screen dims the world to grayscale.

## 7. Items

### 7.1 Item data

`packages/data/items.json`. Fields: id, name, type (`weapon` | `armor` | `helmet` | `shield` | `shoes` | `bracelet` | `necklace` | `earrings` | `consumable` | `material` | `skillbook` | `quest`), subtype, classes allowed, levelReq, size (inventory height 1, 2 or 3 cells), stackable, maxStack, buyPrice, sellPrice, baseStats, upgradeChain (stats per +0 to +9), iconKey, spriteLayerKey (visual layer for equipment), rarity.

Starter content: at least 4 weapon tiers per class (levels 1, 10, 20, 30), 3 armor tiers per class, helmets, shields, shoes, bracelets, necklaces, earrings, red potions (S/M/L, heal HP), blue potions (S/M/L, MP), upgrade materials (Wolf Fang, Bear Claw, Spirit Shard, Corrupted Crystal), a Blessing Scroll, an Enchant Scroll, a Reinforce Scroll, a Return Scroll (warp to town).

### 7.2 Item instances

Every item instance in the database has a unique UUID, ownerCharacterId, location (`inventory` | `equipment` | `warehouse` | `ground` | `shop`), slot, quantity, upgradeLevel (0 to 9), bonuses (array of up to 5 `{ type, value }`), createdAt, createdBy (drop/shop/admin/craft). **Never** duplicate a UUID. All item-changing operations run in a database transaction and write to `item_log`.

### 7.3 Inventory and equipment

- Inventory: **2 pages of 5 columns x 9 rows** (page 2 unlocks at level 20 or is always open, record your choice). Items occupy 1 to 3 vertical cells. Drag and drop, swap, split stacks (Shift+drag), right-click to use/equip, drag outside to drop on ground (with confirm for valuable items).
- Equipment slots: weapon, armor, helmet, shield, shoes, bracelet, necklace, earrings.
- Warehouse (storage NPC in town): 3 pages, shared by all characters of the account.
- Tooltips show all stats, upgrade level, bonuses, requirements (red if not met), sell price.
- Item name color: white (no bonuses), blue (1 to 2 bonuses), purple (3 to 4), gold (5 bonuses).

### 7.4 Upgrading (the blacksmith)

NPC Blacksmith in town. Drag an item in, see required gold + materials + success chance.

| From | To | Chance | Gold | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| +0 | +1 | 95% | 1k | none |
| +1 | +2 | 90% | 2k | none |
| +2 | +3 | 85% | 4k | none |
| +3 | +4 | 70% | 8k | 1 tier material |
| +4 | +5 | 60% | 15k | 2 tier materials |
| +5 | +6 | 50% | 30k | 3 tier materials |
| +6 | +7 | 40% | 60k | 2 Spirit Shards |
| +7 | +8 | 25% | 120k | 4 Spirit Shards |
| +8 | +9 | 15% | 250k | 1 Corrupted Crystal |

- On failure, the item is **destroyed**.
- With a **Blessing Scroll** in the inventory (option checkbox), failure instead drops the item by one level and consumes the scroll.
- Upgrade result has a short suspense animation (1.5 s glowing hammer) then a big success flash or a crack-and-shatter effect with sound. Upgrades of +7 and higher announce a server-wide message on success.
- Use cryptographically secure randomness on the server for all rolls.

### 7.5 Bonuses

- **Enchant Scroll:** adds random bonuses to an item with fewer than 5, replacing all existing ones with 1 to 4 new random bonuses.
- **Reinforce Scroll:** adds one more random bonus if the item has fewer than 5.
- Bonus pool per item type in `packages/data/bonuses.json`, for example: Max HP +500, Attack Speed +5%, Move Speed +10%, Crit Chance +5%, Strong vs Monsters +10%, Strong vs Animals +10%, Strong vs Spirits +10%, Defense +30, HP Regen +10%, Resist Sword/Dagger/Bow/Magic +5%.

### 7.6 Shops and economy

- NPC shops in town: General Goods (potions, scrolls), Weaponsmith, Armorer. Data in `packages/data/shops.json`.
- Currency: **Gold**. Sell items to any shop for the item's sell price.
- Gold sinks: upgrades, potions, teleports, guild creation.

## 8. Skills

- Data in `packages/data/skills.json`: id, class, doctrine, name, description, iconKey, unlockLevel, mpCost (per level), cooldownMs, castTimeMs, type (`melee_aoe` | `projectile` | `area` | `buff` | `heal` | `dash`), range, radius, damage scaling per level, effect (stun, slow, knockback, poison, buff stats), animationRow (LPC row), particle preset.
- Skill levels 1 to 20 with skill points (1 point per level from level 5). Phase 8 adds Master levels M1 to M10 with **skill books** (dropped by spirit stones, a reading has a chance to fail and a 12 h cooldown per skill, configurable).
- Each doctrine has 6 skills, unlocked over levels 5 to 30. Make them distinct and fun, for example:
  - Warrior Blade: Whirlwind (spin AoE), Charge (dash forward, knockback), Sword Burst (cone), Berserk (attack speed buff), Ground Slam (stun AoE), Blade Aura (damage buff).
  - Assassin Shadow: Shadow Step (teleport behind target), Ambush (high damage from stealth), Poison Blades (DoT buff), Fan of Knives (AoE), Stealth, Evasion.
  - Design the rest of the classes in the same spirit and record them in `packages/data/skills.json`.
- Skills must look great: unique particle effects, lights, screen shake for big ones.

## 9. Social systems

- **Chat channels:** Local (900 px radius), Shout (whole map, own kingdom only, 15 s cooldown, level 15+), Whisper (`/w Name message`), Party, Guild, System messages. Chat history 100 lines per tab, tabs: All, Local, Shout, Party, Guild, Whisper. Profanity filter list in data. Messages from players of **other kingdoms** in Local chat appear scrambled (letters shuffled) unless the reader is in a neutral map, which reinforces kingdom identity.
- **Party:** up to 8 members, invite by clicking a player or `/invite Name`. Shared XP within 1500 px with a bonus (+10% per extra member), loot rule: round robin or free for all. Party member HP bars in a side panel.
- **Trade:** right-click player -> Trade. Both sides place items and gold, both must click Lock then Accept. Any change unlocks both. Executed in one database transaction. Logged.
- **Guilds** (phase 8): create at level 30 for 500k gold, up to 32 members, ranks (leader, officer, member), guild chat, guild name shown above players.
- **Friends list** (phase 8): add/remove, online status, whisper from list.
- **PvP mode** toggle on the character: Peaceful (cannot attack players), Kingdom (can attack players of other kingdoms in PvP-allowed maps), Free (can attack anyone in PvP-allowed maps, name turns red). Villages are always safe zones.

## 10. Technical architecture

### 10.1 Monorepo

```
/
  apps/
    client/        Phaser 4 game + React 19 UI overlay (Vite)
    server/        Colyseus 0.17 game server + Express REST API
    admin/         React 19 admin panel (Vite + Tailwind + shadcn/ui)
  packages/
    shared/        types, Zod message schemas, constants, formulas, movement, pathfinding, collision grid
    data/          JSON game data + Tiled maps + JSON schemas validating the data files
  scripts/         generate-maps.ts, seed.ts, validate-data.ts
  docker/          docker-compose.dev.yml (Postgres), docker-compose.prod.yml, Dockerfiles
  AGENTS.md  GAME_DESIGN.md  PROGRESS.md  DECISIONS.md  ASSETS.md
```

### 10.2 Server

- Colyseus server with Express. One room type per map (`map_ember_village`, `map_ember_fields`, ...). Each map can run multiple room instances = **channels** (CH1 to CH4); channel picker on the character select screen and in the system menu. Max 200 players per channel.
- Map transfer (warp): server saves the character, creates a seat reservation in the target map room (same channel), sends it to the client, client leaves and joins with a short fade-to-black loading screen.
- Room state (Colyseus Schema): players, monsters, ground items, NPCs, stones. Keep per-entity fields minimal (id, x, y, dir, anim, hp, maxHp, level, name, kingdom, equipment visual keys). Private data (inventory, gold, XP, stats points) goes only to the owning client via `StateView` or direct messages.
- Systems as separate modules, called from the room tick in fixed order: `input -> movement -> ai -> combat -> regen -> spawns -> drops -> persistence`.
- Use the command pattern for player actions so they are testable without a network.
- **Persistence:** load character on join, autosave dirty characters every 60 s, save on leave, save immediately (transaction) for trades, upgrades, shop purchases, and item pickups of rarity purple+.
- **Auth:** REST endpoints `/api/auth/register`, `/api/auth/login` (argon2 password hashing, JWT access token 1 h + refresh token), `/api/characters` CRUD. Room `onAuth` verifies the JWT and that the character belongs to the account. One active session per account (new login kicks the old one).
- **Rate limiting:** per-client message rate limit (for example 30 messages/s), chat rate limit, login attempt limit per IP.
- Logging with pino. Structured logs for errors and security events.

### 10.3 Database (PostgreSQL + Drizzle)

Tables at minimum: `accounts` (id, email, username, passwordHash, kingdom, role: player | gm | admin, banned_until, ban_reason, created_at, last_login), `characters` (id, account_id, name, class, doctrine, gender, hair, level, xp, gold, hp, mp, stats, stat_points, skill_points, map, x, y, channel, pvp_mode, playtime, created_at), `character_skills`, `items` (see 7.2), `parties` are runtime only, `guilds`, `guild_members`, `friends`, `item_log`, `gold_log`, `chat_log`, `admin_log`, `announcements`, `sessions`.

`scripts/seed.ts` creates an admin account (credentials from `.env`) and a test player.

### 10.4 Client

- Phaser scenes: `Boot` (load manifest) -> `Preload` (progress bar) -> `Login` (React screen) -> `CharacterSelect` (React) -> `World` (Phaser) with React HUD overlay.
- React HUD: HP/MP/XP bars, hotbars, minimap (rendered from collision layer + entity dots), chat, target frame (target name, level, HP bar), party frames, buff icons, windows (inventory, character, skills, shop, blacksmith, trade, warehouse, party, guild, settings, world map).
- Zustand store is the bridge between Phaser and React. Phaser writes game state into the store; React dispatches actions that call a `network` module. React never touches Phaser objects directly.
- Settings: master/music/SFX volume, show names, show damage numbers, weather on/off, camera zoom 1x/2x/3x, key bindings.
- Audio: background music per map, SFX for hits, crits, swing, footsteps (subtle), pickup, level up, upgrade success/fail, UI clicks. Use placeholder WebAudio-generated sounds if files are missing, same manifest approach as art.
- Performance budget: 60 FPS with 100 visible entities on a mid-range laptop. Pool sprites, damage numbers and particles. Use texture atlases for real art.

### 10.5 Admin panel (`apps/admin`)

Login with an account whose role is `gm` or `admin`. Served on its own port in dev and under `/admin` in production. Pages:

1. **Dashboard:** online players total and per map/channel, server uptime, memory/CPU, registrations today, gold in economy, chart of online players over the last 24 h.
2. **Live Rooms:** embed the official `@colyseus/monitor` (mounted at `/colyseus` on the game server, protected by the same admin authentication) in an iframe tab.
3. **Players:** search accounts and characters; view details (stats, inventory, equipment, warehouse with item tooltips); edit level, gold, stats, position; teleport to town; kick; ban with duration and reason; unban; give item (with upgrade level and bonuses); remove item.
4. **Game data:** view and edit monsters, items, drop tables, shops, spawn groups in forms with validation. Changes are saved to the database override table and hot-reloaded by the server without restart (JSON files remain the defaults).
5. **Announcements:** send an immediate server-wide message, or schedule recurring ones.
6. **Logs:** searchable item log, gold log, trade log, chat log, admin action log, with filters by character, date and item UUID (essential for tracking duplication).
7. **Server controls:** set XP rate and drop rate multipliers (events), spawn a monster or boss at a location, schedule a maintenance shutdown with countdown messages to players (graceful shutdown that saves everyone).

Every admin action is written to `admin_log`. In game, GM accounts get commands: `/goto x y`, `/warp Name`, `/summon Name`, `/spawn monsterId [count]`, `/item itemId [count]`, `/level n`, `/invisible`, `/kick Name`.

### 10.6 Testing and tooling

- Vitest unit tests for formulas, inventory placement (multi-cell items), stacking, upgrade logic, drop rolls (seeded RNG), trade, XP/level-up, movement step and pathfinding.
- Colyseus room tests (`@colyseus/testing`) for join, move, attack, pickup.
- `scripts/validate-data.ts` checks all JSON data against schemas and cross-references (every dropTableId exists, every itemId exists). Runs in `pnpm test`.
- ESLint + Prettier. GitHub Actions workflow: install, typecheck, lint, test, build.
- Phase 10: load test with `@colyseus/loadtest` simulating 150 bots walking and attacking on one channel.

### 10.7 Deployment (phase 10)

- `docker-compose.prod.yml`: postgres (volume), server (Node), client and admin built as static files served by a small nginx container. Designed to run behind an existing reverse proxy (nginx-proxy-manager) with WebSocket support. Document required proxy settings in `DEPLOY.md`.
- Environment variables documented in `.env.example`.
- Database migrations run automatically on server start.
- Graceful shutdown: save all characters, notify players.

## 11. Game feel checklist

Apply these everywhere; they matter as much as features:

- Hit flash (sprite tinted white for 60 ms) and tiny knockback (8 px) on every hit.
- **Hit stop:** 40 ms freeze of attacker and target animation on crits and skill hits.
- Screen shake: small on crits, medium on spirit stone destruction and big skills. Respect a setting to disable.
- Damage numbers pop, rise and fade; multiple hits stack slightly offset so they don't overlap.
- Monsters play a hurt animation and a short death animation (fade + sink + small particle puff), never just disappear.
- Item drops pop out with a little arc, bounce once, then show their rarity beam.
- Gold pickup: coin sound + "+1,234 Gold" text near the player.
- Level up: light pillar, sound, full heal, a center-screen banner.
- Attack swing animation starts instantly on key press (client-side), never waits for the server.
- Cursor changes: sword over monsters, hand over NPCs, bag over items.
- Smooth UI: windows open with a 120 ms scale/fade, buttons have hover and press states.
- Loading screens with random gameplay tips.

### Phase 0: Foundation
Monorepo with pnpm workspaces, all apps and packages created, TypeScript strict configs, ESLint/Prettier, Vitest, Docker Compose for Postgres, `.env.example`, `pnpm dev` runs everything with hot reload, `PROGRESS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `ASSETS.md` skeletons, asset manifest + procedural placeholder system, CI workflow.
**Acceptance:** `pnpm install && pnpm dev` opens a Phaser 4 page showing a placeholder character on a generated tile ground, the Colyseus server answers on its port, the admin app shows a login page, `pnpm test` and `pnpm typecheck` pass.

### Phase 1: Walk together
Map generator script producing Ember Village + Ember Fields in Tiled JSON; client renders the map with all layers and y-sorting; server builds collision grid from the same file; guest join (temporary name); click-to-move with A*, WASD movement; client prediction and reconciliation for own player; interpolation for others; StateView interest management; camera follow; local chat with chat bubbles above heads; warp between village and fields; FPS/ping debug overlay (F3).
**Acceptance:** two browser tabs see each other moving smoothly with no jitter, collisions work identically on client and server, chat works, warping works, simulated 150 ms latency (add a dev flag) still feels smooth for your own character.

### Phase 2: Accounts and characters
Register/login REST API, JWT, argon2, kingdom choice, character create/select/delete with LPC-style layered placeholder appearance, character saved and loaded (position, map), one session per account, logout, React login and character select screens styled per section 2.
**Acceptance:** register, create a character, walk, log out, log in, character is where it was. Second login kicks the first session.

### Phase 3: Combat and monsters
Monster data, spawn groups from `mob_zone` objects, monster AI state machine with sleep, pack aggro, space-bar arc attack hitting multiple targets, click-to-attack, damage formulas, hit/miss/crit, HP/MP, regen, death and respawn options, XP, level up, stat points and character window, target frame, all game-feel items from section 11 that apply to combat. Warrior and Assassin (Shadow) basic attacks.
**Acceptance:** a new character can grind from level 1 to 10 on the fields, combat feels punchy, monsters leash correctly, tests for formulas pass.

### Phase 4: Items and economy
Item data and instances in DB, drops with ownership, pickup and auto-loot, multi-cell inventory with drag/drop/split, equipment slots with **visual layer changes** on the character, tooltips, consumables and hotbar, NPC dialog system, shops (buy/sell), warehouse, gold, item_log/gold_log, return scroll.
**Acceptance:** kill monsters, loot items and gold, equip a new weapon and see it on the character, buy potions and use them from the hotbar, sell loot, store items in warehouse and retrieve on another character of the same account.

### Phase 5: Admin panel
Everything in section 10.5 except hot-reloadable game data editing, plus GM in-game commands.
**Acceptance:** admin can log in, see live players and the Colyseus monitor tab, find a character, give it a +5 sword that appears in the player's inventory live, ban a player (who is kicked and cannot log in), send an announcement that appears in game.

### Phase 6: Spirit stones, skills, parties
Spirit stones with wave spawns and special drops, all 4 classes with doctrines and 6 skills each (levels 1 to 20), skill window, skill effects and particles, buffs/debuffs with icons, party system with shared XP and party frames, field boss with respawn timer.
**Acceptance:** a party of two can break a spirit stone together, both get XP, skills look and feel distinct per class.

### Phase 7: Upgrades, bonuses, trading
Blacksmith upgrade system with suspense animation, blessing scroll, server-wide announcements for +7 and up, enchant and reinforce scrolls with bonus pools applied to stats, colored item names, player-to-player trade window, full logging, admin game-data editing with hot reload.
**Acceptance:** upgrade an item to +4 with real failure chances (verified by tests with seeded RNG), add bonuses, trade an item and gold between two players safely, every action appears in admin logs.

### Phase 8: Social and progression depth
Guilds, friends list, whisper/shout/party/guild chat tabs, scrambled cross-kingdom chat, PvP modes and safe zones, skill books and Master skill levels, day/night cycle and weather, minimap and world map.
**Acceptance:** create a guild, invite a friend, fight a player from another kingdom in a PvP map, read a skill book.

### Phase 9: The wider world
All maps from section 3.1 generated with distinct looks per kingdom and biome, channels CH1 to CH4 with picker, monsters and items up to level 60, the Spider Cave instanced dungeon with a boss, mounts (summon item, speed boost, mounted visuals), teleporter NPC between maps.
**Acceptance:** travel from home village to the neutral maps, switch channels, complete the dungeon with a party.

### Phase 10: Polish, performance and deployment
Audio pass, settings window with key rebinding, credits screen, loading tips, performance profiling to meet the budget in 10.4, load test with 150 bots, Docker production setup, `DEPLOY.md`.
**Acceptance:** game holds 60 FPS with 100 visible entities, server tick stays under 20 ms with 150 bots, production stack starts with one command.
